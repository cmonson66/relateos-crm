NectarPay Setup

Get your terminal live in 5 steps

Step 1 of 5
1
Open the Coin
Your wallet is on a physical coin. Let's get it into your phone.
On your phone, open Beekeeper
  • Go to beekeeper.money
  • Tap Scan coin, then Start camera
  • Allow camera permission
  • Scan the laser-etched QR code under the sticker (not the sticker itself)
Sticker trouble? A fingernail lifts it. Rubbing alcohol or WD40 loosens stubborn ones. Get the whole sticker off — nothing abrasive ever.
Agree and create your password
  • Read the four rules on screen and tap to agree
  • Create a password and save it in your password manager, not on paper
  • Tap allow when the phone asks for camera again
Keep it safe. The coin is the money. Lose the coin and the written backup, and the funds are gone forever. That's also what makes it impossible for anyone to freeze or claw back what you earn.
2
Create Your Account
On your computer, set up your merchant dashboard.
Go to nectar-pay.com
  • Click Start free (top right)
  • Use your email or Google account
  • Business name exactly as customers know it (it prints on receipts)
📱
A QR code will appear.
Keep this page open. You'll need it in step 3.
3
Link Your Wallet
Connect your Beekeeper wallet to your NectarPay account.
Back on your phone in Beekeeper
  • Find Link under the TOTAL BALANCE title
  • Tap Link
On your computer
  • The camera opens. Point it at your phone screen
  • Back on your phone, a list of wallets appears
  • Tap approve, then link at the bottom
Enable standard stablecoins
  • Your computer says "linked"
  • Click enable standard stablecoins
  • Take the defaults (unless you asked for something else)
Wait for it. The wallet takes a couple minutes to read as ready. Don't move forward until it does, or the next step won't find it.
4
Install the Terminal App
Get the NectarPay POS app on your terminal device.
On your terminal or Android tablet
  • Open Chrome
  • Go to app.nectar-pay.com/pos-apk
  • Download and tap the file
  • Tap Install
  • Allow NectarPay POS to install itself under Settings → Install unknown apps
  • Delete any old home-screen shortcut — two identical icons mean one can't print
Already installed? Look in the app drawer first. Most Senraise terminals come with it pre-loaded.
5
Test a Payment
Run one real payment to confirm everything works.
On your terminal app
  • Open the NectarPay POS app (the real app, not the shortcut)
  • Go to Store settings → Share Links
  • Create a share link
  • Open it and run a real payment (a couple dollars)
  • Watch it settle while you're there
Set up how it fits your POS
  • Every POS is different (Toast, Square, Clover, etc.)
  • Create a custom payment type called Crypto (NectarPay)
  • Ring the sale on your POS under that payment type, just like delivery
  • Nothing writes into your system automatically — your staff rings it manually
That first live payment is what makes it real. It proves the app is working, the terminal is paired, and the wallet is linked. You're done.
🎉
You're Live
Your terminal is ready to accept crypto. Customers scan, funds land in your wallet in seconds.
Before you go
  • Your exchange account is the one that gets forgotten. Without it, you can't reach dollars. Check it's actually open, not just started.
  • The coin lives in a safe, not the drawer
  • The password is in your password manager
  • Everyone who works the register does one transaction themselves
  • Set up the seven-day check-in on your calendar before you leave
Cashing out works like this: Network fees are per transfer, not per dollar. Bitcoin costs a few dollars a transfer, no matter the amount. Stablecoins on cheap networks don't. Batch your movements — weekly or monthly, never after each sale. A shop with small tickets should leave crypto off and use stablecoins only.
